About the film

THE VOICE OF MYSTERY

The Voice of Mystery is an upcoming short film by Rohith R. Five friends encounter a mysterious voice recorder, and what begins as curiosity leads them toward unsettling questions, unexpected discoveries, and a mystery that tests their friendship.

This upcoming horror, mystery, and thriller short film also explores friendship and moments of comedy. Visit this official website for story details, cast and crew information, trailer updates, gallery images, and release news.
